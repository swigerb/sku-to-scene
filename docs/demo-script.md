# 15-minute executive demo script

## Recorded browser walkthrough

The browser demo replays existing recordings; it does not generate video or run fresh evaluations.
It is a **curated technical reference preview**: eight featured fictional-demo presentations and six learning
cases with known limitations ([curated scope v1](../demo-cache/evaluations/curated-release-v1.json)). No example
is approved for a customer campaign, and the six learning cases still need replacement.
Paused-player posters are still frames extracted at one second from those recordings, not new generated images.
Optionally open **Watch original overview v1 · 2:54** for the captioned introduction, then choose **Start guided demo**.
The original v1 overview is unchanged and uses its embedded captions only; transcript and downloadable captions remain available below it.
Its statements about the original eight clips are historical, not claims about the curated preview.
Use **Next** and **Back** deliberately, with **Play/Pause** beside the recording; the guide does not advance on a timer.
Previewing a draft does not choose it. Explicitly choose **concept #2** to continue to its available recorded
hero; concepts #1 and #3 have no recorded hero.

Open **Technical evidence** to inspect model routing, original QA and recorded evaluations separately.
Keep source screening, current fictional-demo disposition and customer-campaign approval distinct.
Northwind's selected silent derivative does not carry its native source's audio mismatch as an active audio
defect; the original finding remains in history. If human-review evidence is unavailable, treat every clip as not approved.
Return to the guide or explore the scenario gallery without implying that cached playback is a live cloud run.

### Recommended presenter path (featured examples first)

1. **Start guided demo** opens Northwind Coffee: compare the three drafts, explicitly select concept #2, then play
   the silent presentation derivative. Say that native audio was removed, not approved; the native original's
   audio finding stays in **Source history and limitations**.
2. In **Scenario gallery**, stay with the featured cards: Fabrikam (approved-image Kling lookbook), Tailspin or
   Coho (fast social/local volume), Zava (silent generic-layout derivative; its targeted evaluation is
   **partial**), Proseware (realistic office, with its background variance stated) and Munson (localized basket).
3. Optionally open **Learning cases and known limitations** in a category and choose **Inspect learning case** to
   show what review caught. Present the defect as unresolved, not as a fixed example.
4. Close in **Technical evidence**: 8 featured presentations, 6 learning cases and 6 rejected replacement attempts
   (none selected) are different populations, not a total.

Live generation is optional, deliberate and billable; use it only in the CLI pitch below, never to repair the
browser demo on stage.

### Four area guides

The home category tiles open the matching Scenario gallery category; **Start guided demo** (`#guide`) still opens
Northwind Coffee. Gallery cards lead with a recorded poster and **Preview**, not research. Each preview opens
one recording in a dialog; closing pauses it and returns focus to the card. Public sources, prompts and
alternative recordings are in collapsed details. **Open guided demo** starts the exact fictional example at
Brief with a fresh selection, even after viewing it before. Browser history may retain the current guide step;
returning to the gallery restores the category, scroll position and originating-card focus.
Phones use a labelled category selector.
The preview's Close control stays reachable while details scroll. Explicit alternative previews play the
requested recording and return to the title; they do not choose a guide concept or grant approval.
The compact **Change example** control chooses one fictional account; it groups all 14 as **Featured
presentations** and **Learning cases** and does not add another permanent row of area tabs. Every default entry,
including `#guide/<area>`, opens a featured example. The gallery shows featured cards first; learning cases sit in
a collapsed section in their category, with **Inspect learning case** instead of **Open guided demo**. Every
account keeps its direct walkthrough at `#guide/<area>/<account>`; a learning case states its role and unresolved
defect there. Changing accounts clears the previous selection.

Northwind follows **Brief → Concepts → Recorded result → Review**. Concepts has one active player and
three thumbnails from the existing recordings. Watching or changing a preview never selects it; only the
separate **Select concept #2** action unlocks the recorded result. All other examples have one available
recording and follow **Brief → Recorded result → Review**, without inventing a draft/hero relationship.
Text-only examples, including the patio, reach review of their preview recording, not a fictional PIM hero.

The Brief shows the product reference when one exists, business need, channel and short creative summary.
Full prompts, citations, route and cost live in **Brief details**. Screening/evaluation specifics and historical
attempts are expandable. One publication-readiness message stays visible; opening details never rewrites a
verdict. Explicit step changes bring the new step into view and focus its heading, even after scrolling through
long evidence. Playback never changes the step or scroll position. **Options** holds Restart and Fullscreen.
The sun/moon control and the existing live/CLI pitch are unchanged.
On phones, concept choices and selection precede the portrait player. Silent recordings disable Sound.
Review separates **Historical source screening**, **Current fictional-demo disposition** and **Customer-campaign
approval** (not approved), plus proposed review guidance, without an
approval action. Technical evidence shares one example/clip selector across routing, QA and evaluations;
**All attempts, including withheld** exposes the complete audit. Account coverage, the original-eight baseline
and all-attempt totals remain distinct. The footer's **About this demo** holds the full disclosure and public sources.
**Across the demo** lists the six learning cases requiring future remediation, separately from historical source findings. Original screening
scores and thresholds stay separate from additional evaluator/review findings. The **What-if estimate**
calculator never edits retained evidence; **Reset to recording settings** restores the selected recording.

## CLI and agent pitch

**Setup:** GitHub Copilot app open on this repo, with the Squad team loaded (`@retail-merchandiser`,
`@foundry-router`, `@brand-qa`). Before you start:

- Model picker: **Auto → Fusion (High)**, if Fusion is available in your org.
- `SKU2SCENE_MOCK_LATENCY_SCALE=1` for realistic pacing, or `0.1` to move quickly.
- A terminal canvas open with `sku2scene` as the fallback.
- `demo-cache/` clips ready to play.
- Live pacing: the measured 8-second Kling hero took about **281 seconds end to end**, not 15-60 seconds.
  Start it early and use the authentic cached render to keep the 15-minute pitch on time.

**Honesty rule:** say "simulated preview" for any clip whose provenance is `simulated`. Say "estimate" for
every price. The original eight checked-in clips are authentic, but only two passed automated screening. Open the
manifest's recorded QA evidence when offline; without configured evaluators, a new run returns manual review.
The live bounded re-roll also failed prompt adherence and stopped at its cap; do not describe it as an approved fix.
Live steps are optional, deliberate and **billable**; the cached fallback is the default and needs no cloud calls.

## Presenter prompt (Step 2)

> We're launching a fall pumpkin cold brew LTO for a national coffee chain. Generate 3 rapid storyboard concepts
> using our lowest-cost fast models in Microsoft Foundry, evaluate prompt adherence, and then render the best
> concept in high fidelity with native audio using our approved hero cup image.

The agents use the MCP tools `lto_draft_concepts` → (presenter picks) → `lto_render_hero(select=2)`.
`run_lto_demo` is the one-shot rehearsal version only.
