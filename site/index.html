About

About this demo

SKU to Scene is a recorded fictional technical/reference preview: eight featured presentations and six learning cases explore product imagery, creative briefs, model routing and review.

It does not generate video live or approve customer campaigns. Curation is not full remediation: recorded findings, verdicts and unresolved limitations remain evidence, not publication permission.

Processing and governance

Every product clip is an authentic Microsoft Foundry render recorded earlier; silent presentations are identified derivatives with native originals retained. The original v1 narrated overview is an edited compilation; its statements about the original eight clips are historical. No live generation or evaluations run here.

fal models on Foundry are a preview, processed outside Microsoft facilities under fal's terms. Automated screening is an aid: a person must sign off before anything is published. Read the governance caveats before planning a scoped pilot.

Fictional brands and products. Real company names identify representative retail segments and cite public sources only; no affiliation, endorsement, trademarks, or customer data. Product images are AI-generated. Prices are preview list prices. MIT licensed.
